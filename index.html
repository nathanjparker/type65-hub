      How the tax is figured

      One rate on everything is a fair estimate, because tax follows where the parts are delivered and the car is titled, not where they ship from. Big retailers charge your home rate at checkout; whatever arrives untaxed (often the kit and small vendors) is usually billed at the same rate as use tax when you title the car. Picking a state fills in its average state-plus-local rate; type your own city's rate over it. Rough numbers, not tax advice.
