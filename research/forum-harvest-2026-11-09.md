### Body Fitting PAGE 2 (#41–80)
- Hinge brackets measure ~83.5° (PNWTim, kit end of 2024) and ~98° (Indy Shu) — not 90; if they lay flat you don't need wedges; light-cup/bracket misalignment (~3/16" gap one side) common; option: elongate slot or shim with 1/4" delrin
- Hood opening: must notch the underside of the nose for A/C condenser and radiator (stair-step relief per edwardb); nose only lifted ~20" before contact; open angle ~41° at pontoon lip, back corner ~40" above pontoon (Indy Shu); overcut relief ~1/4" to do it once; strut mount "9 inches back from shock mount" dimension questioned but PNWTim found manual strut dimensions work
